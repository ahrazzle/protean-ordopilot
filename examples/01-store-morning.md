Owner's notebooks before: `store-stock` ("Milk, eggs, bread orders for the corner store."), `store-staff` ("Saturday rota and cover."), `home-chores` ("Bins, bulbs, small home jobs.").

Transcript:

> **Owner:** Milk low, eggs low, bread low. Order by Thursday.
> **Orda:** Filed under `store-stock`. Same list as yesterday, now updated.
> **Pick:** CONTINUE `store-stock` — words match the stock summary; confidence high.

Why it continued: same topic, same words as the stock summary. One list stays current. Nothing new was opened.
