# Example 2 — New supplier question opens a new thread

Same notebooks as Example 1, plus past supplier notes under `store-supplier` ("Price sheets and rep quotes.").

Transcript:

> **Owner:** New rep from FreshLine. Milk 10 cents cheaper, eggs 5 cents dearer. Worth a switch?
> **Orda:** New question. Opened `store-supplier-freshline`. Old price notes stay filed next to it.
> **Pick:** NEW thread — novel topic (fresh rep, switch question); low match with any one notebook.

Later, comparing stays easy: the new sheet sits beside the old notes. The owner reads both and picks.
